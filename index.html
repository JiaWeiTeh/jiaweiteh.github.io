About

I am a PhD candidate in astrophysics at [Institute], [University], supervised by [Supervisor]. I study how feedback from young massive star clusters — stellar winds, radiation and supernovae — shapes the molecular clouds that form them, and how far that feedback can carry before it stalls. I develop TRINITY, a semi-analytic code that follows a feedback-driven bubble through its evolutionary phases to its stopping fate.

Research

Feedback-driven bubbles in molecular clouds

For a given giant molecular cloud mass, star-formation efficiency, density profile and ambient medium, TRINITY integrates the expansion of the feedback bubble — shell radius, velocity, thermal state and force budget — through its energy- and momentum-driven phases, and resolves whether the shell escapes the cloud, stalls, or recollapses.
